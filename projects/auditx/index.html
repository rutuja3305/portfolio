01

Problem & Intent

Manual review does not scale

Reviewing financial transactions by hand is slow, and risky patterns are easy to miss. AuditX looks at vendor payments in government fund allocation scenarios and surfaces the ones that deserve a closer look.

02

How the System Works

Analyse, score, flag, review

Transactions are analysed, a risk-prediction model scores them, and anomaly detection highlights unusual behaviour. Flagged transactions are then opened for review.

  • Detection-oriented workflows for duplicate invoices, inflated payments, potentially fictitious vendors and unusual transaction patterns.
  • Vendor risk scores and flagged-transaction monitoring.
  • Transaction-level review.
01 · INGESTVendor transactions
02 · PREDICTXGBoost risk score
03 · DETECTAnomaly detection
04 · FLAGSuspicious transactions
05 · REVIEWTransaction-level review
03

Risk Analysis

Vendor risk and suspicious indicators

Each vendor receives a risk score, and individual transactions carry flags that explain why they were raised — for example a high-amount flag. This helps reviewers prioritise where to look first.

04

Engineering & Architecture

Model, interface, data

How the pieces fit together.

  • Risk prediction: XGBoost.
  • Anomaly detection: Isolation Forest, where implemented.
  • Interface: a web interface for monitoring risk scores and managing complaints, built with React, Vite and Tailwind CSS.
  • Data: Supabase integration.
  • Blockchain layer: Solidity smart-contract rules and blockchain logging were part of the AI Grand Challenge entry — see that story for context.
05

Dashboard & Evidence

The real interface

The dashboard counts scanned, flagged, high-risk and clean transactions and streams a live feed. Opening a flagged transaction shows vendor, scheme, amount and detected flags, and lets a reviewer raise a complaint, which then appears in the complaints list as pending review.

06

Limitations & Future Scope

Flags are not findings

A flagged transaction requires investigation. It does not, on its own, establish fraud. The system is a prioritisation aid for human reviewers.
