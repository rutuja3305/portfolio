01

The Problem

Scattered knowledge, slow evidence-finding

Patent guidelines, traditional knowledge resources, government regulations and research relevant to Ayurveda are spread across many different sources.

For a researcher or innovator, finding the evidence and understanding the potential IP considerations can take a long time.

02

The Platform

Major modules

IP-SAKTI Sahayak brings the main workflows into one interface.

  • Ayurveda AI Assistant — a RAG-based assistant that answers from retrieved documents.
  • Knowledge Atlas — educational Ayurveda content to browse and learn from.
  • Innovation Intelligence — explore existing innovations and potential research gaps.
  • Preliminary innovation assessment — patent, traditional knowledge, ABS, regulatory and international considerations.
  • Evidence & source tracking — retrieved-document metadata and retrieval-confidence indicators.
03

The RAG Pipeline

From PDF to grounded answer

Documents are extracted from PDF, split into overlapping chunks and embedded. At query time the most relevant chunks are retrieved semantically and passed to the language model as context, so responses stay grounded in the sources. Source metadata and confidence indicators are exposed in the interface.

04

Architecture

The actual stack

Each layer below is what the project actually uses.

  • Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
  • Backend: Node.js, Express.js, TypeScript
  • Embedding model: Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dimensions)
  • Vector database: Supabase PostgreSQL with pgvector
  • LLM inference: Groq with openai/gpt-oss-120b
05

Current Implementation

A working baseline

A baseline RAG pipeline has been built from a curated collection of patent, Ayurveda, traditional knowledge and regulatory documents. Ingestion identified 56 PDFs; 48 documents were stored after duplicate and skip handling, producing approximately 1,831 chunks and embeddings.

The query pipeline retrieves relevant evidence and passes it to the LLM, and the application shows source metadata and retrieval-confidence indicators.

06

Future Direction

Planned, not yet implemented

The items below are planned improvements and are not part of the current build.

  • Hybrid retrieval and reranking
  • Multilingual support
  • Retrieval-quality and answer-groundedness evaluation
