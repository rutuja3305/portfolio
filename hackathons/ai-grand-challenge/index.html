The Challenge

Blockchain & AI Innovation

The AI Grand Challenge 2026 was organised at MIT Art, Design & Technology University under the theme “Build with Intelligence”, with its Grand Finale on 17 April 2026. Our entry was in the Blockchain & AI Innovation category, where the problem context was financial fraud: how can suspicious transactions be identified before they cause damage?

02

The Project

AuditX

AuditX explores financial transaction risk analysis. It is designed to identify potentially suspicious transactions through risk analysis, supporting review before and after execution.

03

Technical Highlights

Three layers

The entry combined machine learning with blockchain.

  • Isolation Forest-based anomaly detection for fraud-risk scoring.
  • Solidity smart-contract rules for transaction validation.
  • Blockchain logging for transaction integrity and transparency.
